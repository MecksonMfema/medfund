Claims frequency & severity

Annualised claim frequency and severity statistics per scheme × insurance line × currency, clocked by service date. Frequency = claims ÷ exposure member-months; severity percentiles are Postgres-native (mean / median / P95). Exposure uses a static proxy (active members × days ÷ 30.4375) because member-status history is not yet stored, so treat frequency as an indicative trend metric rather than an audit-grade denominator.
