Automated CTC allocations

Rules-driven auto-allocation of cash-to-cardholder payments — e.g. auto-split contributions across enrolled members at month-end.

Back to pending
Backend not yet available. Automated CTC allocation rules require an auto-CTC rules controller on the finance service, and hook-up to the contribution-lifecycle Kafka topic. Neither ships yet — this page will surface the rule editor and run history once they do.

Manual flow (available today)

Record CTC allocations by hand under New CTC payment. Pending rows appear under Pending CTC payments until you commit them — committed rows post to the beneficiary's balance and drop into Committed CTC payments.

What the rules engine will do

  • Trigger on contribution.committed events per tenant.
  • Auto-split into per-member CTC allocations by an operator-configured formula.
  • Skip members flagged excluded (waiver, terminated, out-of-scheme).
  • Batch commit at a schedule (nightly, weekly, or on-demand).
  • Emit a run summary to the operator inbox on each execution.
